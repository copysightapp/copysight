CopySight turns text visible anywhere on your Mac into editable text. Press Control-Command-2, drag over a screen region, and paste the recognized result.

Recognition runs locally with Apple Vision. Screenshots are never saved or uploaded. CopySight has no account, analytics, network service, subscription, or background capture.

Use CopySight to copy text from images, videos, protected PDFs, presentations, remote desktops, and any app that does not let you select its text.

Features:
• One global shortcut for screen text capture
• Automatic, English, or Spanish recognition
• Optional language correction and preserved line breaks
• Repeat the previous region without drawing it again
• Automatic clipboard copy and last-result recovery
• Configurable shortcut and launch at login
• Native support for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs

CopySight is a one-time purchase. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases, accounts, ads, or tracking.
