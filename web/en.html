Privacy policy

Screen capture

CopySight uses macOS Screen Recording permission only when you start Capture Text or Capture Previous Selection. It captures the pixels inside the region you draw, or the same previous region. It excludes its own interface and captures no audio or pointer.

Local processing

The captured image stays in memory only while Apple Vision converts it to text on your Mac. It is never written to disk, retained after recognition, uploaded, or sent to a server.

Recognized text

The recognized text remains temporarily in the app's memory so you can copy it again and, when enabled, is placed on the macOS clipboard. CopySight does not persist it to disk. macOS and other apps may access clipboard content according to their own behavior and privacy policies.

No collection or sharing

CopySight has no backend, network code, account system, advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not collect, receive, store, sell, or share screen pixels, captured images, recognized text, clipboard content, or usage data with any third party.

Questions before installing

Does CopySight upload screenshots?

No. Screen capture and OCR run locally with ScreenCaptureKit and Vision. Captured images are not saved.

Why is Screen Recording permission required?

macOS protects screen pixels. CopySight needs that system permission only to capture the region you draw.

Which languages work?

Automatic detection is the default. English and Spanish can also be selected explicitly for more predictable correction.
