# Code of conduct

CopySight is committed to a respectful, focused, and harassment-free project community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and viewpoints.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project's native macOS OCR scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form. Include links or screenshots needed to understand the incident, but do not share unrelated private information. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and as promptly as possible.

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents CopySight.
